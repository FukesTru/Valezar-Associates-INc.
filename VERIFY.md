### Tax and legal figures (please review each one)

35. Florida has no state personal income tax.
36. A C corporation doing business in Florida files Florida Form F-1120.
37. Florida's state sales tax is 6%, and Miami-Dade and Broward each add a county surtax.
38. Most Florida employers pay reemployment tax and file a report every quarter.
39. Form 941 is filed every quarter and Form 940 once a year.
40. W-2s are due to employees and the Social Security Administration by January 31.
41. Form 1099-NEC is generally due to contractors and the IRS by January 31.
42. Florida employers must report each new employee to the Florida New Hire Reporting Center.
43. Federal estimated tax payments are due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15, moving to the next business day when a date falls on a weekend or holiday.
44. Calendar year partnership (1065) and S corporation (1120-S) returns are due March 15. C corporation (1120) returns are due April 15.
45. Personal returns are due April 15. An extension moves filing to October 15, but tax owed is still due April 15. A business extension gives more time to file, not to pay.
46. S corporation owners who work in the business must pay themselves a reasonable salary.
47. Common small business retirement plans are the SEP IRA, SIMPLE IRA, 401(k) and solo 401(k).
48. Farm income goes on Schedule F.
49. People serving in a combat zone can get extra time to file and pay federal income tax.
50. A buyer can give a supplier a Florida Annual Resale Certificate instead of paying sales tax on goods bought for resale.
51. Someone who moved to Florida during the year may still owe a return to the state they left.
52. Section 7216 of the Internal Revenue Code limits how tax return preparers may use and share tax return information (privacy policy).
