The credential

What is an Enrolled Agent?

An Enrolled Agent, or EA, is a tax professional who has earned the right to represent taxpayers before the IRS. A person becomes an Enrolled Agent by passing a 3-part IRS exam, or through qualifying experience as an IRS employee. Enrolled Agents must keep up with continuing education to keep their status.

In practice, that means Mirtha can speak for you with the IRS on audits, collections and appeals, and help you answer the letters that come in between.

What an Enrolled Agent can do for you

  • Represent you before the IRS on audits
  • Handle IRS collection matters with you
  • Represent you in IRS appeals
  • Prepare business and personal tax returns
  • Answer IRS letters and notices
