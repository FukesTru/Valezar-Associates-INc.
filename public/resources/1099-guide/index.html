1099 forms, a plain guide for Miami businesses

A 1099 is an IRS form that reports money paid to someone who is not an employee. Valezar & Associates Inc., an accounting and tax firm in Miami, FL, prepares 1099s for its clients. This guide explains who usually needs a Form 1099-NEC, what to collect before you pay a contractor and when the forms are due.

Who gets one

Who gets a Form 1099-NEC?

Form 1099-NEC reports payments for services to people and businesses who are not your employees, such as independent contractors and freelancers.

Payments made in 2026For payments made in 2026 and later, you generally file a 1099-NEC when you pay a contractor $2,000 or more in the year.

Payments made in 2025For payments made in 2025, the amount was $600 or more.

Business payments onlyThe form covers payments you make in the course of your business, not personal payments.

Most corporationsPayments to most corporations are not reported on Form 1099-NEC, with exceptions such as legal fees.

Step by step

How do you handle 1099s without the January rush?

Most of the work happens before the first payment.

  1. Collect a W-9 first

    Ask every contractor for a Form W-9 before you pay them. It gives you their legal name and tax ID number.

  2. Track payments

    Record each payment, and how you paid it, in your books through the year.

  3. Check the list in December

    Total each contractor’s payments and see who crosses the amount for the year.

  4. File by January 31

    Send each 1099-NEC to the contractor and file it with the IRS by January 31.

Questions

Questions about 1099s

Do I send a 1099 to a corporation?

Usually not. Payments to most corporations are not reported on Form 1099-NEC. There are exceptions, such as payments to attorneys, so ask us if you are unsure.

What if I paid a contractor by credit card or a payment app?

Payments made by credit card, or through a payment app that processes card payments, are reported by the payment company on Form 1099-K. You do not report them on Form 1099-NEC.

What if a contractor will not give me their tax ID number?

You may have to withhold federal income tax from their payments, which the IRS calls backup withholding. Ask for a Form W-9 before the first payment to avoid this.

Do I have to file 1099s electronically?

If you file 10 or more information returns of any kind in a year, the IRS requires you to file them electronically.

Does Florida need a copy of my 1099s?

Florida has no state personal income tax, so there is no Florida income tax filing for these forms.
