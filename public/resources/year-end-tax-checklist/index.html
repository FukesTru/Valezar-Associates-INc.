Year end tax checklist for Miami business owners

A year end tax checklist is a short list of things to review before December 31, while there is still time to act. Valezar & Associates Inc., an accounting and tax firm in Miami, FL, put this list together for business owners. Work through it with your accountant, and not on your own.

Your books

Are your books ready for year end?

Every other step depends on knowing where the year stands.

  • Reconcile every bank and credit card account through the latest month.
  • Clear out uncategorized transactions and fix anything posted to the wrong account.
  • Collect receipts for large purchases, and keep a clear scan or photo of each one.
  • Look at the profit and loss for the year to date, so you know what you are planning around.

Planning moves

What can you still change before December 31?

These choices can change this year’s tax. Ask before you act, because each one also affects cash.

  • Timing. Whether to send invoices or pay bills before or after December 31.
  • Equipment. Whether a purchase you already need makes sense this year or next.
  • Owner pay. If you own an S corporation, whether your salary through payroll is reasonable for the work you do.
  • Retirement plans. Whether to open or fund a plan. Some plans must be opened by December 31, and others can be opened later.
  • Estimated taxes. Whether your payments so far cover what you are likely to owe. The fourth quarter payment is due January 15.

January forms

What do you need for January filings?

W-2s and 1099-NEC forms are due January 31. Gather what they need now.

  • Collect a Form W-9 from every contractor you paid this year.
  • Confirm each employee’s address and Social Security number for their W-2.
  • List every contractor payment, and how it was paid, for 1099 reporting.
  • For charitable gifts of $250 or more, keep the written acknowledgment from the charity.

Questions

Questions about year end planning

When should I start year end planning?

Start in the fall. October and November leave time to act on what you find, and most moves have to happen by December 31.

Should I buy equipment before December 31 to save tax?

Sometimes. A purchase can lower this year’s tax, but it still costs the business cash. Ask us before you buy, so we can show you the effect on both.

Do I need receipts for charitable gifts?

Yes. For any single gift of $250 or more, you need a written acknowledgment from the charity. Keep it with your records.
