Month by month

Which tax dates come up each year?

The dates that matter most for Florida businesses and families.

  1. January 15

    Fourth quarter federal estimated tax payment for the year before, for individuals.

  2. January 31

    W-2s to employees and the Social Security Administration. 1099-NEC to contractors and the IRS. Form 941 for the fourth quarter and the yearly Form 940. Florida reemployment tax report for the fourth quarter.

  3. March 15

    Partnership returns on Form 1065 and S corporation returns on Form 1120-S, with K-1s to owners, for calendar year businesses.

  4. April 15

    Personal returns on Form 1040 and C corporation returns on Form 1120. First quarter estimated tax. Extension requests. Last day for most IRA contributions for the year before.

  5. April 30

    Form 941 and the Florida reemployment tax report for the first quarter.

  6. May 1

    Florida annual report for corporations and LLCs on Sunbiz. Florida corporate income tax return, Form F-1120, for most calendar year C corporations.

  7. June 15

    Second quarter federal estimated tax payment.

  8. July 31

    Form 941 and the Florida reemployment tax report for the second quarter.

  9. September 15

    Third quarter federal estimated tax payment. Extended partnership and S corporation returns.

  10. October 15

    Extended Form 1040 personal returns and extended C corporation returns.

  11. October 31

    Form 941 and the Florida reemployment tax report for the third quarter.

  12. December 31

    Last day of the tax year. Most tax planning moves for the year have to happen by now.

Questions

Questions about tax deadlines

What happens when a deadline falls on a weekend?

When a federal deadline falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, it moves to the next business day.

When is Florida sales tax due?

Florida sales tax returns are due on the 1st of the month after the reporting period and are late after the 20th. Some businesses file monthly, others quarterly or yearly, depending on how much tax they collect.

Does an extension give me more time to pay?

No. An extension gives more time to file the return. Any tax you owe is still due by the original deadline.

Do these dates apply to every business?

They apply to calendar year businesses and individuals. If your business uses a fiscal year, or has a special situation, your dates can differ. Ask us about yours.
