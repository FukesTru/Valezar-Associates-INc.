Step by step

How does a new client get started?

From the first call to your first month with us.

  1. Reach out

    Call, text or send the form on this page. Tell us in a few words what you need.

  2. First conversation

    We talk in person, by phone or by video about your business, your records and your deadlines.

  3. Records review

    We look at recent tax returns, statements or reports to see where things stand.

  4. Proposal and fee

    We explain what we would do and what it costs, and you sign an engagement letter.

  5. Getting set up

    You get access to our secure online platform and we start on the work.

Before the first conversation

What should you have ready?

You do not need everything. Bring what you have, and we will ask for the rest.

For a business

  • The last 2 years of business tax returns
  • Recent financial statements, or the name of your accounting software
  • A list of bank accounts, credit cards and loans
  • Recent payroll reports, if you have employees
  • Your entity documents and federal employer ID number
  • Any letters from the IRS or the Florida Department of Revenue

For a personal return

  • Last year’s tax return
  • W-2 and 1099 forms for this year
  • Records of other income, such as a side business
  • Mortgage interest and property tax statements
  • Any letters from the IRS

Keeping it safe

How do we keep your information safe?

We use a secure online platform for documents, signatures and reports. Please do not send Social Security numbers, tax ID numbers or bank account numbers by regular email or text message. We will give you a secure way to send them.

Secure platformDocuments and signatures move through a secure online platform, from upload to filing.

MeetingsYou can meet us at our office in Kendall, by phone or by video.

Every monthMonthly clients get reconciled accounts and financial statements every month.

Every quarterComplete package clients get a quarterly video meeting to review key performance measures.

Questions

Questions about getting started

Can you work with my current QuickBooks file?

Yes. We work in QuickBooks, so we can usually pick up from the file you already have. We look at it during the first review and tell you if anything needs fixing.

What if I have a tax deadline coming up?

Tell us on the first call, and give us the date. Deadlines decide what we look at first.

Will you text me?

Only if you agree to it. Clients who agree may get texts about appointments, billing and payroll, such as a reminder to send hours. Reply STOP to any text to stop them.

Do I have to meet in person?

No. You can meet us at our office in Kendall, by phone or by video, whichever suits you.
