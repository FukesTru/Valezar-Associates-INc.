Privacy policy

Privacy policy

This privacy policy explains how Valezar & Associates Inc., an accounting and tax firm in Miami, FL, handles information you share through this website, by phone or by text message. We use your information to answer your enquiry and to serve you as a client. We do not sell it.

Last updated November 10, 2026

Who we are

Valezar & Associates Inc. is an accounting and tax firm at 12485 SW 137th Ave., Ste. 206, Miami, FL 33186. You can reach us at (305) 252-5505 or admin@valezar.com.

What information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us. When you send the form or use the chat on this website, that is the details you enter, such as your name, phone number and message. When you call, text or email us, we receive the details you share.

When you become a client, we collect the tax and financial information needed to do the work you have engaged us for. That information is handled under your engagement letter and the professional rules that apply to tax practitioners. Federal law, including section 7216 of the Internal Revenue Code, limits how tax return preparers may use and share tax return information.

What this website collects on its own

The contact form and the chat window on this website are provided by LeadConnector, the platform we use to manage client messages. What you enter in the form or the chat goes to us through that platform, so we can reply by phone, text or email. The form and the chat may use cookies and record basic details, such as the page you were on, that they need to work and to show us where your enquiry came from. We do not add advertising cookies or tracking pixels to this website. Our website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiry and talk with you about our services
  • To provide the accounting, payroll and tax services you engage us for
  • To send appointment reminders, follow-up messages and billing messages
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations

Text messages

Valezar & Associates Inc. sends text messages only to people who have agreed to receive them. The messages are about your enquiry and, if you become a client, about appointments, billing and payroll. Agreeing to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or of using our services.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving text messages, or reply HELP for help. You can also call us at (305) 252-5505. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Our full SMS terms and conditions are further down this page and in our terms of use.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us do our work, such as our secure document platform, accounting and payroll software, and email and phone providers, and only as needed for that work. We also share information when the law requires it, or when you ask us to, for example with the IRS on your behalf. This sharing never includes text message opt-in data or consent, as the next section explains.

Mobile information and text message consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service and message delivery, is permitted. All other categories of information sharing in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How we protect your information

We use a secure online platform for client documents and signatures. Please do not send Social Security numbers, tax ID numbers or bank account numbers through the website form, regular email or text message.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Some records must be kept for a period of time under tax and professional rules, and we will tell you when that applies.

Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 through this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

SMS terms and conditions

Valezar & Associates Inc. is committed to protecting your privacy and we make every effort to respect your wishes and personal information. In keeping with this commitment, we have established the following policy for the use of our SMS services, the Valezar & Associates Inc. text messaging program.

SMS consent communication

The information (phone numbers) obtained as part of the SMS consent process will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Types of SMS communications

If you have consented to receive text messages from Valezar & Associates Inc., you may receive messages related to the following.

  • Appointment reminders
  • Follow-up messages
  • Billing inquiries
  • Payroll reminders

Example

Valezar & Associates: Hello, this is a friendly reminder of your upcoming appointment at 12485 SW 137th Ave., Ste. 206, Miami, FL 33186 on 4/15/25 at 10am. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messaging at any time.

Message frequency

Message frequency may vary depending on the type of communication. For example, you may receive up to 2 SMS messages per week related to your appointments, billing, payroll and similar matters.

Example

Valezar & Associates: Good afternoon Mr. Jones, this is a friendly reminder to submit the hours for this week’s payroll. Please send the hours to payroll@valezar.com by 5pm today. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messaging at any time.

Potential fees for SMS messaging

Please note that standard message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier’s pricing plan. These fees may vary if the message is sent domestically or internationally. If you have questions about your text or data plan, please contact your wireless provider.

Opt-in method

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from Valezar & Associates Inc. in the following ways.

  • Verbally, during a conversation
  • By submitting an online form and checking the SMS consent box
  • By filling out a paper form

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Opt-out method

You can opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time. To do so, simply reply “STOP” to any SMS message you receive. After you reply STOP, we will send one SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed, and you will no longer receive SMS messages from us. Alternatively, you can contact us directly to request removal from our messaging list. If you want to receive SMS messages again, sign up the same way you did the first time.

Help

If you are experiencing any issues, you can reply with the keyword HELP. Or, you can get help directly from us by calling (305) 252-5505, by email at admin@valezar.com, or at our contact page.

Carrier liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Additional options

If you do not wish to receive SMS messages, you can choose not to check the SMS consent box on our forms.

Standard messaging disclosures

  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out at any time by texting “STOP.”
  • For assistance, text “HELP” or visit our contact page.
  • Message frequency may vary.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Our terms of use set out the same text messaging terms.
