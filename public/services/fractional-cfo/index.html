What you get

What does a fractional CFO do for a small business?

We give you the right financial information at the right time, in language a business owner uses. Before each meeting we prepare reports built around your business, then we go through them with you.

A KPI, or key performance indicator, is a number you track to see if the business is on course, like profit per job or revenue per member.

What our CFO service covers

  • Budgeting
  • Revenue analysis
  • Expense analysis
  • Payroll analysis
  • Cost of goods sold analysis, meaning the direct cost of what you sell
  • Credit card fee analysis
  • Cash flow analysis
  • Industry KPI analysis
  • Tax planning and projections

Signs it is time

When is it time to bring in a fractional CFO?

Owners usually reach for CFO help at one of these moments.

Sales are up, but cash is tight

Revenue is growing and you still worry about making payroll. A cash flow review shows where the money goes and when it comes back.

A big decision is coming

You are thinking about hiring, leasing space or buying equipment. We model what it does to profit, cash and taxes before you commit.

You are not sure what to charge

Prices were set years ago. Revenue, expense and cost of goods analysis shows which jobs or products actually make money.

You need numbers for a lender

A bank wants financial statements and projections. We prepare them and walk you through what they say.

Taxes keep surprising you

A tax projection during the year shows what you are likely to owe while you can still plan for it.

Every decision runs through you

You make the calls without a clear view of the numbers. Regular reports give you that view in plain language.

How it works

How does a fractional CFO engagement work?

You get a steady rhythm of reports and meetings, at our office in Kendall or by video.

  1. Gather the numbers

    We start from your books. If we already keep them, there is nothing to send. If not, you give us access to your accounting software.

  2. Build your reports

    We prepare reports on cash flow, profit, expenses, payroll and the KPIs that matter in your industry.

  3. Meet and decide

    We go through the reports with you, answer your questions and talk about the decisions in front of you.

  4. Plan for taxes

    Tax planning and projections are part of the work, so the tax effect of each decision is in view.

What should you do first?

Pull together your last 12 months of financial statements, or note which accounting software you use. Then write down the 3 biggest decisions you face this year. Bring that list to the first conversation.

We work in QuickBooks, and we use reporting tools such as Fathom to turn your numbers into clear reports.

Florida and federal taxes

How do taxes fit into CFO planning in Florida?

Florida has no state personal income tax, so planning for owners centers on federal tax. The business still has Florida taxes to plan cash for.

C corporationsA Florida C corporation files a Florida corporate income tax return, Form F-1120, in addition to its federal return.

Sales taxA Florida business that sells taxable goods or services collects sales tax and pays it to the Florida Department of Revenue.

Reemployment taxMost Florida employers pay reemployment tax, the state name for unemployment tax, on employee wages every quarter.

Estimated taxesOwners of pass-through businesses usually make federal estimated tax payments 4 times a year.

Questions

Questions about fractional CFO services

What is the difference between a bookkeeper and a CFO?

A bookkeeper records what already happened. A CFO uses those records to plan what happens next, with budgets, cash forecasts and tax projections. Valezar & Associates offers both, so your CFO advice can be built on books we keep.

Do I need a fractional CFO if I already have an accountant?

Many accountants only see your numbers once a year, at tax time. A fractional CFO works with you during the year, while there is still time to change course. If you are happy with your current tax preparer, tell us and we will talk about how the two roles fit together.

How often will we meet?

That depends on your business and what you need. Some owners want a monthly review and others prefer a quarterly one. We agree on the schedule before we start.

Can a fractional CFO help me get a loan?

We can prepare the financial statements and projections a lender usually asks for, and help you understand them before the meeting. No one can promise what a lender will decide.

Will you look at my credit card processing fees?

Yes. Credit card fee analysis is part of our CFO service. We look at what you pay to accept cards and show you where the cost comes from.
