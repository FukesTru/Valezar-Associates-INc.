Business tax preparation

Which business tax returns do we prepare?

We prepare federal and Florida returns for the most common business types. When we also keep your books, the return is built from records we already know.

See how bookkeeping makes tax time easier
  • Form 1120-S for S corporations
  • Form 1065 for partnerships and LLCs with 2 or more members
  • Form 1120 for C corporations, plus the Florida Form F-1120
  • Schedule C for sole proprietors and single-member LLCs, filed with the owner’s personal return
  • 1099 forms for the contractors your business paid

Deadlines

When are business tax returns due?

These dates apply to businesses that use the calendar year.

March 15Partnership returns on Form 1065 and S corporation returns on Form 1120-S are due March 15.

April 15C corporation returns on Form 1120 are due April 15.

ExtensionsA business tax extension gives more time to file the return. It does not give more time to pay.

Personal tax preparation

How does personal tax preparation work with us?

We prepare Form 1040 returns for individuals and families, including business owners and people who are self-employed. Taxes do not have to feel daunting. The whole process is digital and secure, from upload to signature.

  1. Upload your documents

    Send your tax documents through our secure online platform.

  2. We prepare and review

    We look at your situation, the deductions that apply and the credits you qualify for.

  3. You review and sign

    You review the finished return and sign it electronically.

  4. We file

    We file electronically and stay available for questions all year.

What should you send?

  • Last year’s tax return
  • W-2 and 1099 forms
  • Business income and expense records, if you are self-employed
  • Mortgage interest statements, Form 1098
  • Records of charitable gifts
  • Child care costs and the provider’s details
  • Health insurance Form 1095-A, if you bought coverage on the Marketplace
  • Any letters from the IRS

IRS letters and audits

What if the IRS sends you a letter?

Most IRS letters ask a question or point out a difference. Do not ignore one, and do not reply before you understand it. Send us a copy and we will explain what it says and what to do next.

Mirtha C. Almanzar is an Enrolled Agent, which means she is enrolled to practice before the IRS. She can represent you on audits, collections and appeals. We also help with notices from the Florida Department of Revenue, so you can stay focused on your business.

Florida returns

How do Florida taxes affect your return?

Florida has no state personal income tax, so most Florida residents file only a federal income tax return. If you moved to Florida during the year, you may still owe a return to the state you left.

April 15Personal returns on Form 1040 are due April 15 for most people.

October 15An extension moves the personal filing deadline to October 15, but tax owed is still due April 15.

How much does tax preparation cost?

The fee depends on the return and the forms it needs, and we tell you the fee before we start. For business clients, tax preparation is included in each of our monthly accounting packages.

Questions

Questions about tax preparation

Do you file returns electronically?

Yes. We file your return electronically once you have reviewed and signed it.

Will you make sure I get the biggest refund?

We make sure you claim the deductions and credits you qualify for. No one can promise a refund amount, because it depends on your income, your withholding and your situation.

Do you help after my return is filed?

Yes. We are available all year for tax questions. If the IRS sends a letter about a return we prepared, send it to us.

Do I need to come to the office?

No. You can upload your documents, review your return and sign it online. You are also welcome at our office in Kendall.

Should I file an extension?

An extension can help if you are waiting on documents. It gives you until October 15 to file a personal return, but any tax you owe is still due April 15. Talk to us before the deadline.

What if the IRS sends me a letter?

Do not ignore it, and do not pay or reply before you understand it. Send us a copy. We explain what it says and what to do next, and Mirtha C. Almanzar, EA, can represent you before the IRS.
