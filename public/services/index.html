Services

Accounting and tax services for Miami businesses and families

Valezar & Associates Inc. is an accounting and tax firm in Miami, FL. We offer fractional CFO services, tax planning, monthly bookkeeping, payroll, and business and personal tax preparation. We work with small businesses, and with individuals and families, across Miami-Dade and Broward counties.

Fractional CFO

Do you need CFO advice without a full time CFO?

A fractional CFO is a part-time chief financial officer. We meet with you on a regular schedule to go over cash flow, profit, budgets and taxes, and we turn the numbers into plain answers for your next decision.

See how our fractional CFO service works

What it includes

  • Budgets and cash flow analysis
  • Revenue, expense and payroll analysis
  • Cost of goods and credit card fee analysis
  • Industry KPI tracking
  • Tax planning and projections

Tax planning

Do you want to plan your taxes before the year ends?

Tax planning means looking at your income, business structure and timing while there is still time to act. We look at deductions, retirement plans and how you pay yourself, and we show you the likely tax effect before you decide.

See how tax planning works

What it includes

  • Business structure and S corporation questions
  • How owners pay themselves
  • Retirement plan options
  • Estimated tax payments
  • Year end planning

Monthly bookkeeping

Do you need someone to keep your books every month?

We record and categorize every transaction, reconcile your bank and credit card accounts, and close each month with financial statements you can use. Bookkeeping is billed at a fixed monthly fee.

See what monthly bookkeeping includes

What it includes

  • Daily transaction classification
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Bills, payments and invoicing
  • Monthly financial statements and budget reports
  • 1099s for contractors

Payroll

Do you need payroll done right and on time?

We run your scheduled payrolls, set up new employees, deposit payroll taxes and file the payroll tax returns that go with them. Employees can see their paystubs online.

See how our payroll service works

What it includes

  • Scheduled pay runs and employee setup
  • Payroll tax deposits and returns
  • W-2s and 1099s
  • Florida reemployment tax reports
  • Garnishment and pension remittance

Tax preparation

Who prepares business and personal tax returns?

We prepare returns for S corporations, partnerships, LLCs and C corporations, and Form 1040 returns for individuals and families. The process is digital from document upload to e-signature, and we file electronically.

See how tax preparation works

What it includes

  • Form 1120-S, 1065 and 1120 business returns
  • Florida corporate income tax returns
  • Schedule C for sole owners
  • Form 1040 personal returns
  • Help with IRS letters after filing

More ways we help

What else do business owners bring to us?

What if the IRS sends you a letter?

Send us a copy before you reply. We explain what the letter says and what to do next. Mirtha C. Almanzar is an Enrolled Agent, so she can represent you before the IRS on audits, collections and appeals. We also help with letters from the Florida Department of Revenue.

Read about IRS letters and audits

Are you starting a new business?

We help you choose an entity type, prepare the state and federal forms, set up your accounting system and compare retirement plans for you and your team. Starting with clean books makes every later year simpler.

Read about help for new businesses
