How much do your services cost?

Ongoing work, such as bookkeeping, payroll and CFO services, is billed at a fixed monthly fee. The fee depends on the size and needs of your business. We explain it before any work begins, so you are never surprised by an hourly bill.

What is an Enrolled Agent?

An Enrolled Agent is a tax professional enrolled to practice before the Internal Revenue Service. Mirtha C. Almanzar, the owner of Valezar & Associates, is an Enrolled Agent. That means she can represent you before the IRS on audits, collections and appeals.

Do I have to come to your office?

No. You can upload documents, review your return and sign it online through our secure process. You can also meet us at our office at 12485 SW 137th Ave., Ste. 206, in Miami.

Do you work with businesses outside Miami?

Yes. We work with businesses and families across Miami-Dade and Broward counties, including Kendall, Doral, Homestead, Weston and Fort Lauderdale.

Do you only work with businesses?

No. We work with business owners, and we also prepare personal tax returns for individuals and families.
