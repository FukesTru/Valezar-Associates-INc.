Medical and dental practices

How do we help South Miami medical and dental practices?

South Miami has many medical offices, a lot of them near South Miami Hospital. A practice has two sets of needs, the practice’s books and payroll, and the owner’s own taxes. We handle both.

See how our payroll service works

What a practice usually needs

  • Payroll for front desk and clinical staff, with W-2s at year end
  • 1099s for contractors the practice pays
  • Monthly books that keep practice income apart from the owner’s spending
  • Tax planning for the owner, including a retirement plan for the practice
  • A business tax return and the owner’s Form 1040, prepared together

Local rules

Does South Miami issue its own business tax receipt?

South Miami is its own city, so its businesses deal with both the city and the county.

A city of its ownSouth Miami is an incorporated city in Miami-Dade County, with its own city government.

Two receiptsA business in South Miami generally needs a City of South Miami business tax receipt and a Miami-Dade County local business tax receipt.

DowntownDowntown South Miami runs along Sunset Drive (SW 72nd Street) near US 1, also called South Dixie Highway.

Working together

How do South Miami clients work with us?

Most of the work happens online. You upload documents through our secure platform and sign returns electronically. When you want to meet in person, head west on Kendall Drive (SW 88th Street) or Killian Parkway to SW 137th Avenue.

  • Monthly bookkeeping closes each month with reconciled accounts and statements.
  • Payroll pays your team and files the payroll taxes on schedule.
  • Fractional CFO reviews cash flow and profit with you on a regular schedule.
  • Tax planning looks at your structure, pay and retirement plan before year end.
  • Tax preparation covers the business return and your personal return.

Questions from South Miami clients

Do you work with South Miami medical and dental practices?

Yes. Medical and dental practices are one of the kinds of businesses we know best. We handle payroll for staff, monthly books for the practice and tax planning for the owner.

Does South Miami issue its own business tax receipt?

Yes. South Miami is an incorporated city, so a business there generally needs a City of South Miami business tax receipt and a Miami-Dade County local business tax receipt.
