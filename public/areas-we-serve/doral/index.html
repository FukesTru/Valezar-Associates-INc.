Local and state rules

Which tax rules matter most for Doral businesses?

Three facts that shape the books of a Doral trade company.

Resale certificatesA Florida business that buys goods to resell can give its supplier a Florida Annual Resale Certificate instead of paying sales tax on those goods.

Two receiptsDoral is an incorporated city, so a Doral business generally needs a City of Doral business tax receipt and a Miami-Dade County receipt.

Gross profitCost of goods sold is the direct cost of the products you sell, and it is subtracted from sales to find gross profit.

Questions from Doral clients

Do you work with import, export and wholesale companies in Doral?

Yes. We help trade and wholesale businesses track inventory and cost of goods sold, keep sales tax records straight and run payroll for warehouse and office staff.

What is a Florida Annual Resale Certificate?

It is a certificate from the Florida Department of Revenue that lets a business buy goods for resale without paying sales tax on them. The business gives a copy to its supplier.

Does Doral issue its own business tax receipt?

Yes. Doral is an incorporated city, so a Doral business generally needs a City of Doral business tax receipt and a Miami-Dade County local business tax receipt.
