Homestead businesses

Which Homestead businesses can we help?

Homestead sits between the Redland farm country and two national parks, and its businesses reflect that.

Farms and nurseries

Growers in the Redland have seasonal income, equipment and workers to track. Farm profit or loss goes on Schedule F of the owner’s return.

Contractors and builders

Contractors building across south Miami-Dade need job costs, payroll for crews and 1099s for subcontractors.

Businesses that serve visitors

Shops, restaurants and tour businesses near Everglades and Biscayne national parks need sales tax and seasonal cash flow handled.

Families and military households

Families connected to Homestead Air Reserve Base and across the city need their personal returns done right.

Local and federal rules

Which tax rules matter most in Homestead?

Three rules that come up often in south Miami-Dade.

Farm incomeFarm income and expenses are reported on Schedule F, the IRS form for profit or loss from farming.

Two receiptsA business inside Homestead city limits generally needs a City of Homestead business tax receipt and a Miami-Dade County receipt.

Military deadlinesMembers of the armed forces serving in a combat zone can get extra time to file and pay federal income tax.

Questions from Homestead clients

Can I work with you from Homestead without driving to Kendall?

Yes. You can upload documents, review your return and sign online, and we can talk by phone or video. Our office is also a short drive north on the Florida Turnpike.

Do I need a City of Homestead business tax receipt?

If your business is inside Homestead city limits, you generally need a City of Homestead business tax receipt as well as a Miami-Dade County local business tax receipt.

How is farm income reported?

Farm income and expenses are reported on Schedule F, the IRS form for profit or loss from farming, which is filed with the owner’s personal return.

Do you prepare personal returns for Homestead families?

Yes. We prepare Form 1040 returns for individuals and families in Homestead, including military families.
