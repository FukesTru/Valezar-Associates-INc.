About the firm

What kind of firm is Valezar & Associates?

Valezar & Associates Inc. is an accounting and tax firm in Miami, FL. We offer fractional CFO services, tax planning, monthly bookkeeping, payroll, and business and personal tax preparation.

Who runs Valezar & Associates?

The firm is owned and led by Mirtha C. Almanzar, EA, MST. She is an Enrolled Agent with a Master’s in Taxation.

What is an Enrolled Agent?

An Enrolled Agent is a tax professional enrolled to practice before the Internal Revenue Service. An Enrolled Agent can represent taxpayers before the IRS on audits, collections and appeals.

Where is your office?

Our office is in Suite 206 at Corporate Park at Kendall, 12485 SW 137th Ave., Ste. 206, Miami, FL 33186. We are open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

Fees

How do your fees work?

Ongoing work is billed at a fixed monthly fee, through 3 packages called Basic, Standard and Complete. One-time work is quoted before we start. You know the fee before any work begins.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. Ongoing work is billed at a fixed monthly fee, so you are not surprised by an hourly bill.

Bookkeeping and payroll

What does monthly bookkeeping include?

We classify transactions, reconcile your bank and credit card accounts, process bills and invoices, and prepare monthly financial statements and budget reports.

Which accounting software do you use?

We work in QuickBooks, and we can set it up for a new business.

Do Florida employers withhold state income tax?

No. Florida has no state income tax. Employers still withhold federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and most pay Florida reemployment tax every quarter.

Taxes

When are personal tax returns due?

For most people, Form 1040 is due April 15. An extension moves the filing deadline to October 15, but tax owed is still due April 15.

When are business tax returns due?

For calendar year businesses, partnership and S corporation returns are due March 15, and C corporation returns are due April 15.

Does Florida have a state income tax?

Florida has no state personal income tax. C corporations doing business in Florida file a Florida corporate income tax return, Form F-1120.

Can you promise I will pay less tax?

No one can promise a tax result. We show you your options and the likely tax effect of each one before you decide.

IRS letters

What should I do if I get a letter from the IRS?

Do not ignore it, and do not reply before you understand it. Send us a copy and we will explain what it says and what to do next.

Can you represent me in an IRS audit?

Yes. Mirtha C. Almanzar is an Enrolled Agent, so she can represent you before the IRS on audits, collections and appeals.
